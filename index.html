WHY JAPANESE SCRIPT IS THE LAST MOAT IN AUTHENTICATION
Japanese is the only writing system on earth that simultaneously combines logographic (kanji · 漢字), syllabic (hiragana · ひらがな / katakana · カタカナ), and phonetic annotation (furigana · ふりがな) layers. This multi-dimensional structure creates an authentication space of extraordinary density — one encoded in 2,000 years of cultural memory, not training data. Western AI cannot reproduce it.
